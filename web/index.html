Spherical grains of astronomical silicate and graphite with the dielectric functions of Draine (2003), optics from Mie theory (graphite in the 1/3–2/3 approximation), and PAHs with the cross sections of Draine & Li (2007), heated by the interstellar radiation field of Mathis, Mezger & Panagia (1983) scaled by U plus the CMB. Temperatures follow from energy balance; grains whose temperature fluctuates by more than 1% are treated stochastically with the heat capacities and thermal-continuous method of Draine & Li (2001). Grain shapes, ices, grain charging (the PAH ionized fraction is set by hand), and sublimation are left out.
